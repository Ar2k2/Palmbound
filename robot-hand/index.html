PALMBOUND

Greatness abounds from my generous hands

PLAY

The game

Palmbound is a metroidvania. You are a broken robot hand, crawling alone through a cave. You can barely move. Something down here is waiting for you.

Explore the map, find stations to save your progress, and collect abilities that open up places you couldn't reach before. Dash, glide, slam, cling to ceilings and blast anything in your way.

It runs right in your browser. No download, no sign-up.

Controls

Arrows / WASDMove and climb
MMap
RBack to last station (hold for the start)

More controls unlock as you find abilities. On a phone, touch controls appear automatically.

Work in progress. Palmbound is still being built. Things will change, and saves may not carry over between updates. If it runs slowly, try low quality mode.
